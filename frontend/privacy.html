Mangaice values your privacy. The platform stores your reading progress, chapter, and interface preferences locally on your device via localStorage. Additionally, we may register temporary display names and comments submitted voluntarily by users.

We utilize your browser's local storage capabilities exclusively to enhance your user experience (such as maintaining your dark/light theme choice, tracking scroll progression on chapters, and remembering your commenter name). You can clear these records at any time directly through your browser settings.

3. Cookies and Technical Data

We may use infrastructure and hosting providers (such as Supabase or Cloudflare) that automatically process anonymous technical logs and basic traffic statistics to ensure platform stability, security, and performance.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or transfer your personal preferences or local data to external third parties.
